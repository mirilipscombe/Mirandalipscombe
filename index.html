I’m Miranda, a Bristol-based videographer, photographer and editor with a Film & Television degree from the University of Bristol.

My work takes me from weddings and to dance floors and commercial shoots. I enjoy the full process: planning the shoot, finding the perfect moments and sculpting the final edit.
